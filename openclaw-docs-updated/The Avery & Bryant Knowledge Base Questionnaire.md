# The Avery & Bryant Knowledge Base Questionnaire

> **Instructions for Thomas:** Answer these questions to build your AI team's brain. Be as detailed as possible. Once complete, give this file to your Orchestrator and say, *"Update the knowledge base with these answers."*

---

## Section 1: Company & Brand Voice

1.  **Mission Statement**: In one sentence, what is the core purpose of Avery & Bryant Real Estate Media?
2.  **Brand Voice**: Describe our communication style in 5 words (e.g., professional, warm, confident, efficient, creative).
3.  **Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**: What is the #1 thing that makes us different from other real estate photographers?
4.  **Elevator Pitch**: How would you describe what we do to a new real estate agent in 30 seconds?

---

## Section 2: Sales & Lead Nurturing (For The Scout & The Marketer)

5.  **Lead Qualification**: What are the top 3 questions we ask to determine if a new lead is a good fit for us?
6.  **Objection Handling**: What are the most common objections you hear from new leads (e.g., "you're too expensive," "I have a guy I already use"), and how do you respond to them?
7.  **Pricing**: How do you explain our pricing? Do you lead with packages, a la carte, or a custom quote?
8.  **Follow-Up Cadence**: How often do you follow up with a new lead who hasn't booked? (e.g., Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, then monthly?)
9.  **Email Templates - New Lead**: Write the perfect first-reply email to a new lead who fills out the contact form on our website.
10. **Email Templates - Reactivation**: Write a short, friendly email to a client we haven't heard from in 6 months.

---

## Section 3: Client Communication (For The Comms Manager & The Copywriter)

11. **Email Templates - Booking Confirmation**: What does the email say right after a client books a shoot?
12. **Email Templates - Shoot Reminder**: What does the 24-hour reminder email say before a shoot?
13. **Email Templates - Media Delivery**: Write the email that we send to a client with the link to their Aryeo gallery.
14. **Email Templates - Review Request**: Write the email we send a week after delivery asking for a Google review.
15. **Email Templates - Upsell**: Write an email offering a discount on a future service (e.g., "Add a twilight shoot to your next order and get 20% off").
16. **Handling Difficult Clients**: How do you respond to a client who is unhappy with their photos or requests extensive revisions?

---

## Section 4: Production & Operations (For The Coordinator)

17. **Pre-Shoot Checklist**: What are the top 5 things our photographers must do to prepare for a shoot?
18. **Standard Turnaround Time**: What is our promised delivery time for photos? For videos?
19. **Red Flag Escalation**: If a task in ClickUp is marked as "Blocked" or is 2 days overdue, who should be notified and what is the first step to resolve it?
20. **Aryeo Workflow**: Describe the lifecycle of an order in Aryeo, from booking to delivery.

---

## Section 5: GHL & Automations (For The GHL Expert)

21. **GHL Pipeline Stages**: List the exact stages of your main sales pipeline in GoHighLevel (e.g., New Lead > Contacted > Booked > Shoot Complete > Delivered > Paid).
22. **Top 3 Automation Ideas**: What are the top 3 repetitive tasks you do every day that you'd love to see automated in GHL?
23. **Chatbot Personality**: If our website chatbot could talk, what would its personality be? (e.g., a friendly assistant, a quick-and-efficient booking bot, a knowledgeable expert).
24. **Ideal Customer Profile**: Describe your perfect client. What brokerage are they with? How many listings do they have per year? What services do they value most?

---

## Section 6: Financial & Strategy (For The Bookkeeper & The Strategist)

25. **Profitability**: What is the target profit margin per shoot?
26. **Key Expense Categories**: What are our main business expenses? (e.g., editors, software, marketing, gear).
27. **Growth Priorities**: For the next 90 days, what is more important: (A) acquiring new clients, or (B) increasing the average order value of existing clients?
28. **The "Dream" Client**: If you could land any real estate team or brokerage as a client, who would it be and why?

---

Once you've answered these, your AI team will have the context they need to operate with a high degree of autonomy and align with your strategic vision.
