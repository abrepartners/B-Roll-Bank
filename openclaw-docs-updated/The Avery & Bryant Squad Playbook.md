# The Avery & Bryant Squad Playbook

> **A practical guide for Thomas on how to use your 10-agent AI team effectively.**

---

## The Core Principle: Delegate Outcomes, Not Tasks

Your job is to act as the CEO. You set the direction, and your AI team handles the execution. Instead of telling them *how* to do something, tell them *what* you want to achieve.

**Bad command:** *"Go to GHL, find all contacts with the #new-lead tag, and send them an email with our pricing PDF."*

**Good command:** *"Marketer, send the pricing guide to all new leads from the last 24 hours."*

Your Orchestrator knows how to translate that outcome into a series of actions for the right agent.

---

## Daily & Weekly Rhythms

Here is a simple, effective cadence for managing your AI team:

### Daily (5 minutes)

**Command:** `"Run the Daily Pulse."`

This single command triggers your `HEARTBEAT.md` and spawns three agents:

1. **The Analyst** — Pulls GHL revenue data from the last 24 hours.
2. **The Coordinator** — Scans ClickUp for Red Flag tasks.
3. **The Comms Manager** — Checks Gmail for urgent messages and drafts replies.

The Orchestrator will synthesize their findings into a single, concise report so you know exactly what happened in the last 24 hours.

### Weekly (15 minutes)

**Command:** `"Run the Weekly Business Review."`

This triggers the second part of your `HEARTBEAT.md` and spawns three different agents:

1. **The Bookkeeper** — Generates a weekly revenue report from Stripe, comparing it to your $14,400/week target.
2. **The GHL Expert** — Audits the GHL pipeline for stagnant opportunities and automation gaps.
3. **The Strategist** — Reviews the week's performance against the Master Operational Canvas and recommends the top priority for the week ahead.

The Orchestrator will deliver a strategic brief that tells you where you are, where you need to go, and what to focus on next.

## Best Use Cases for Each Agent

Here are practical, outcome-focused commands for each of your 10 agents:

| Agent | When to Use | Example Command |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Scout** | A new lead comes in from any channel. | `"Scout, a new lead just called from 555-123-4567. Qualify them and get them booked."` |
| **The Coordinator** | You need to know the status of a shoot. | `"Coordinator, what's the status of the 123 Main St shoot? Are we on track for delivery?"` |
| **The Comms Manager** | You get an email you don't want to answer yourself. | `"Comms Manager, draft a reply to the email from Jane Doe about rescheduling her shoot."` |
| **The GHL Expert** | You want to automate a repetitive task. | `"GHL Expert, I want to automate a 3-day follow-up for all new leads. Design the workflow."` |
| **The Analyst** | You have a question about revenue or KPIs. | `"Analyst, what's our average order value for the last 30 days?"` |
| **The Bookkeeper** | You need to check on payments or invoices. | `"Bookkeeper, which invoices are still outstanding from last week?"` |
| **The Marketer** | You want to run a marketing campaign. | `"Marketer, draft a reactivation campaign for all clients we haven't heard from in 90 days."` |
| **The Copywriter** | You need a message written for a specific purpose. | `"Copywriter, write a short, punchy email announcing our new drone video service."` |
| **The Designer** | You need a visual asset created. | `"Designer, create a new Instagram post graphic for our latest cinematic video feature."` |
| **The Strategist** | You're thinking about the future of the business. | `"Strategist, based on our current numbers, what's the single biggest opportunity for revenue growth next month?"` |

## The Knowledge Base: Your Team's Brain

Your agents are only as smart as the knowledge you give them. The **Knowledge Base Questionnaire** (the next file I'm creating) is designed to extract your expertise and turn it into SOPs, email templates, and canned responses.

**How to use it:**

1. Answer the questions in the questionnaire.
2. Give the answers to your Orchestrator.
3. **Command:** `"Update the knowledge base with these answers."`

Your Orchestrator will then distribute the relevant information to each agent, making them smarter and more autonomous.

This playbook provides the structure. The Knowledge Base provides the intelligence. Together, they allow you to run your business from the top down, with a full AI team executing your vision.
