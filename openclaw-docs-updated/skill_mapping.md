# Avery & Bryant Skill Mapping

Based on the Master Operational Canvas, the following skills will be created to support the Orchestrator Pattern in OpenClaw.

| Business Engine | Sub-Agent | Skill Name | Purpose | Key SOPs Covered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engine A** | The Scout | `lead-capture-pro` | Automate lead intake and qualification. | SOP 1: Lead Capture |
| **Engine B** | The Coordinator | `production-manager` | Manage shoot scheduling, prep, and delivery. | SOP 2, 3, 5: Scheduling, Production, Delivery |
| **Engine C** | The Copywriter | `client-growth-nurture` | Handle follow-ups, reviews, and upsells. | SOP 6: Follow Up & Repurposing |
| **Engine D** | The Analyst | `ops-revenue-tracker` | Track KPIs, revenue, and operational health. | KPI Control Panel, Daily Pulse |
| **Engine E** | The Strategist | `business-expansion` | Identify new revenue streams and align goals. | Master Operational Canvas Alignment |

## Skill Details

### 1. `lead-capture-pro` (The Scout)
- **Trigger**: New inquiry (form, text, call).
- **Actions**: Capture property details, qualify lead, send booking link.
- **Tools**: CRM, Messaging.

### 2. `production-manager` (The Coordinator)
- **Trigger**: Shoot scheduled or raw files uploaded.
- **Actions**: Assign photographer, send prep checklist, notify editor, deliver final files.
- **Tools**: ClickUp, Dropbox, Calendar.

### 3. `client-growth-nurture` (The Copywriter)
- **Trigger**: Project marked complete.
- **Actions**: Send thank you, request review, offer branding/social media upsells.
- **Tools**: CRM, Email/SMS.

### 4. `ops-revenue-tracker` (The Analyst)
- **Trigger**: Daily schedule or manual request.
- **Actions**: Retrieve GHL revenue, identify ClickUp red flags, calculate weekly KPIs.
- **Tools**: GHL API, ClickUp API.

### 5. `business-expansion` (The Strategist)
- **Trigger**: Weekly review or owner request.
- **Actions**: Analyze revenue vs. $750k target, suggest new digital products or partnerships.
- **Tools**: Master Operational Canvas, Financial Reports.
