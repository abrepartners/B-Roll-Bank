# Graphics Best Practices References

## Platform and Layout

- Instagram carousel visuals generally perform best in portrait feed framing.
- Recommended production target in this project: `1080x1350` (`4:5`) with an inner safe zone for headline overlays.
- Related reference:
  - https://ppc.land/meta-updates-instagram-marketing-api-with-new-carousel-video-and-instagram-user-data-fields/

## Accessibility

- Maintain text contrast at least `4.5:1` for normal text.
- Maintain text contrast at least `3:1` for large text.
- Source:
  - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html

## Visual Hierarchy

- Use clear emphasis so the eye lands on a single focal element first.
- Group related elements and avoid visual noise that obscures CTA.
- Source:
  - https://www.nngroup.com/reports/visual-design/

## Readability

- Keep overlay text concise and avoid long lines on mobile canvases.
- Source:
  - https://baymard.com/blog/line-length-readability

## Prompt Construction

- Strong image prompts include specific context: subject, environment, composition, lighting, color, and constraints.
- Source:
  - https://cookbook.openai.com/examples/generate_images_with_gpt_image
